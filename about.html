About CyberGlitch

CyberGlitch is an educational website designed to help children and young people learn about cybersecurity and online safety in a fun and interactive way.

Through quizzes, challenges, and practical examples, visitors can learn how to identify scams, recognise phishing attempts, protect personal information, and stay safe online.

This project was created as part of an HTML and CSS Bootcamp to develop web design skills while promoting cybersecurity awareness.

Our Mission

CyberGlitch aims to inspire young people to become confident digital citizens by teaching the knowledge and skills needed to navigate the internet safely and responsibly.

What You Will Learn

  • How to create strong passwords
  • How to spot phishing emails
  • How to protect personal information online
  • Safe social media practices
  • How to identify online scams
