Top Cyber Safety Tips

Staying safe online is important. Follow these simple tips to protect yourself and your information.

Password Safety

  • Never share passwords with anyone.
  • Use strong passwords with letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Use a different password for each account.

Email Safety

  • Think before clicking links.
  • Watch out for suspicious emails.
  • Never give personal information through email.

Social Media Safety

  • Be careful with strangers online.
  • Keep your profile information private.
  • Do not share your address or phone number publicly.

Device Safety

  • Keep software updated.
  • Use antivirus protection when possible.
  • Lock your device when not using it.

💡 Cyber Fact

Most phishing attacks look like they come from trusted companies or people. Always check carefully before clicking a link.
