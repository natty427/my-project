Cyber Threat Gallery

Explore examples of common online threats and learn how to recognize them.

📧 Phishing Email

A fake email designed to trick people into sharing personal information.

👤 Fake Profile

Online profiles that pretend to be someone they are not.

💬 Scam Message

Messages that try to steal information or money.

🔓 Weak Password

Passwords that are easy for hackers to guess.

🎮 Play Safe Online

Online games can be fun, but remember not to share personal information such as your real name, address, school, or passwords. Only play and chat with people you trust.

🔐 Online Privacy

Check your privacy settings regularly. Keep personal information private and think carefully before posting photos, videos, or details about yourself online.

🦠 Computer Viruses

A computer virus is a harmful program that can damage files, slow down devices, or spread to other computers. Avoid downloading files from unknown websites.

🔗 Suspicious Links

Not every link is safe. Cybercriminals often use fake websites and shortened links to trick people into revealing personal information. Always check a link before clicking.

🤳 Social Media Safety

Be careful about what you share online. Photos, locations, and personal information can be seen by people you do not know. Always review your privacy settings and think before you post.
